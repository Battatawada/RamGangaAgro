# Imagery

## Now

The site ships with original line drawings in `design/illustrations/`.

- `panicle.svg` — hero media, and the Raktshali page opener.
- `contours.svg` — a quiet field behind the journey section, at low contrast.
- `mark.svg` — favicon and the footer monogram. It is a panicle, not a leaf, not a bowl of rice, not a farmer.

Alt text for these drawings says they are drawings, not photographs of Ram Ganga Agro.

Every media slot is a `MediaHero` or `EditorialImage` with `kind: illustration | image | video`. Swapping kind does not change the layout.

## When photographs exist

The farm supplies real pictures. Stock photography of other farms is not a stand-in.

### Shot list

| Shot | Where it goes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wide view of the land, standing height, morning or late light | Hero, once it replaces the drawing. Also Our Farm | Include sky and field. No logo burned in |
| The same view closer, plants in the foreground | Introduction, optional | |
| Raktshali plants in the field, showing the grain if it is visible | Crop page, crop card | Show the colour the farm confirms. Do not grade an unconfirmed red into the picture |
| A handful or a mound of the dried grain on a neutral surface | Crop page | Paper, wood, or cloth the farm actually uses. No marble studio if that is not the farm |
| Soil, water, or the field edge | The land | |
| Work in the field, only with the person's consent and a release | Our story, practices | Faces optional. Hands and tools are enough |
| Harvest: cutting, gathering, drying, if these happen on the farm | Journey steps | One still per step, square |
| Grain after preparation, as the farm actually prepares it | Preparation | Do not style a restaurant dish and call it the farm |
| Pack, label, or sack, only when a real pack exists | Future product | No mock pack |
| A short, silent, stable shot of the field, 10–20 seconds | Hero video | Poster image required. No music autoplay. Captions if anyone speaks |

### Treatment

Natural light. Colour close to the scene. Paper-white balance, not teal-and-orange. Crop with the subject off-centre. Leave sky or soil as quiet space so type can sit on a scrim later if the hero becomes full-bleed.

Full-bleed photography, when it arrives, gets a paper scrim at the left on desktop so the headline stays `#1C1915` on a light field. Do not put white type on a busy photo.

Video is muted, looped, with a pause control, and `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the poster instead.

## What not to commission

Leaf patterns as a texture. Cartoon farmers. A green circle logo. A sack with a fake "100% organic" badge. Aerial drone shots presented as if they prove a certification.

## Food and rice, specifically

Show the grain's actual colour once the farm has confirmed it. Until that confirmation, do not describe the crop as red in copy or in a retouch. Do not add nutritional callouts on the image.
