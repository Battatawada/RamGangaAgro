# 02 — Reference research

Studied live on 12 November 2026. Traffic is unverified. No analytics source was consulted. Pages were read in a browser, including one phone-sized viewport. Screenshots were used during the review and were not committed, so the repository does not store copies of those sites’ pages. These sites are teachers, not templates. Do not copy their logos, sentences, illustrations, or layouts.

`https://gardensweetfarm.com/` returned HTTP 500 that day. The farm’s working site is `https://www.gardensweet.com/`, which was studied instead.

## Two Brothers Organic Farms

`https://twobrothersindiashop.com/`

Pages opened: homepage, `/pages/our-philosophy`, `/pages/tbof-traceability`, `/blogs/all-blogs`. A full product detail page was not opened. Product cards were read on the homepage. Mobile was inferred from a persistent hamburger at a desktop window, not from a separate phone pass. That is a research limit.

**Visual.** Forest green wordmark, about `rgb(29, 77, 1)`, Gotham. Cream page. A promo ticker in a dark green bar. The hero is a flat illustration: a person, a discount, product packs, and a cartoon landscape. Press logos sit under it. Product cards are photographs of packs with orange badges (Trending, New Launch), a heart, a variant dropdown, and a green Add to Cart.

**Layout.** Centred wordmark, search under it, hamburger rather than a visible menu. The menu holds Ghee, Atta, shop by category, shop by concern, and Farm Life. Farm Life contains founders, a sustainability report, traceability, farm visits, philosophy, lab reports, and blogs. The footer is a company block with a newsletter, categories, and a postal address in Pune.

**Interaction.** Carousels, wishlist, variant select, sticky chat. The search field was open on arrival during this visit. The ticker repeats offers.

**Story.** The homepage sells. The philosophy page tells the story: a full-bleed aerial photograph, the line “Little something about us”, and chapters for soil, farmers, cattle, and community. The traceability page argues for knowing food before it reaches the table, then points at a QR code on the pack and a 2025 timeline. The blog is a stream of cooking and ingredient articles, dated around the festive season when fetched.

**Use.** The chaptered farm story, and traceability as a permanent idea. The QR page is the precedent for `/harvest/[batch]`, later.

**Leave.** The discount hero, the cartoon, the ticker, the badge-covered grid, celebrity quotes, and “Buy now” as the first action.

## Hexhamshire Organics

`https://hexhamshireorganics.co.uk/`

Pages opened: homepage at desktop and at a 390×844 viewport, `/pages/our-story`, `/blogs/anns-journal`, `/pages/contact-us`. The box product URL is `/products/hexhamshire-box`. That product page was not given a full visual pass. That is a research limit.

**Visual.** Background about `rgb(252, 252, 242)`. Body is Poppins. The large headline uses a display face named Grow. Deep green type. One terracotta pill button. A hand-drawn green wordmark. Huge type, wide margins.

**Layout.** Header is a menu button, the wordmark, and a bag, even on desktop. The hero is type only, no photograph. Under it, a postcode checker. Then two green panels (buy the box, a truck icon for free delivery), certification badges, then a split story: a grey empty image box beside an ochre headline, “Lovingly grown on our small farm in Northumberland.” Footer: About, Contact, Deliveries, Privacy, and an email signup titled “Farm updates in your inbox.”

**Mobile.** The same stack, full width, type still large, the pill button and the postcode field spanning the screen. The menu stays a button. This is the mobile pattern to learn: one column, the headline intact, no separate mobile design.

**Story.** The story page names Ann and Bob, 2011, a house, 6.5 acres, Soil Association certification in 2013, four fields, and a weekly journal. Those facts are theirs. Ram Ganga Agro does not borrow the dates or the certification. The journal is letters from Ann, dated, about weather, capacity, and what was harvested. The contact page fetch was thin: a heading and “Fresh Local Organic.”

**Use.** Type scale, paper, one action, a named journal, an honest empty image slot, and a short nav.

**Leave.** The script logo, the truck icon, the certification badge row, the postcode widget above the story, and pill buttons.

## Winterspring Farm

`https://www.winterspringcsa.com/`

Pages opened: homepage, and `/about`, which redirected to `/farm-story`. Article pages were titled on the homepage (“Your Farm Summer: PPE recs…”, “Best Borscht Recipe”) and not opened. That is a research limit.

**Visual.** Header `rgb(146, 183, 85)`. Headlines in Lora. A circular seal. The hero is a photograph of herbs with a white rounded card of type on top: “Fresh organic vegetables.”

**Layout.** Nav: Home, CSA, Markets, Seedlings, Produce Guide, About, plus Instagram, Facebook, and a cart. Sections follow how you get food: CSA, markets, seedlings, a values line, what’s in season, farm news, recipes. The footer has market hours, an address in West Bend, a phone, an email, and a newsletter with first name and email. Credit to Grapevine Local Food Marketing.

**Story.** The farm story page uses a photo banner and a white title card, then a narrow column of type with a round inset photo of people at a stall. It names Caleb and Roxanne and a real history. The homepage can say the next CSA is not open: stay tuned, join the newsletter. That sentence is the useful pattern.

**Use.** Saying plainly when something is not available, and putting the newsletter where a relationship starts. Organising later commerce by the real offer, not by a fake catalogue.

**Leave.** The lime bar, the seal, the wavy section edge, the photo-dependent hero. Without their pictures this layout fails. Ram Ganga Agro cannot start there.

## Garden Sweet

`https://www.gardensweet.com/`

Pages opened: homepage, including “What each season brings”, and `/about`. `https://gardensweetfarm.com/` did not load.

**Visual.** White page. Playfair Display for headlines, Inter and Karla elsewhere. A script logo with a small sun. A terracotta pill for Membership. Full-bleed photographs and video. A slider.

**Layout.** A horizontal nav: Pick-Your-Own, Farm Stand, Plant Nursery, Events, Photography, More. The hero is a photograph with a white card, “Welcome to Garden Sweet!” Under it, a quiet centred introduction. Then a seasonal row: a serif title and three columns of crops. Later, memberships with prices. The footer has an email signup, “Stay in the Loop.”

**Story.** The about page names Amy Kafka, a horticulture degree, and a mission. It lists experiences and a long “what we grow” inventory. The homepage can say sold out for 2026 and closed until spring without looking broken.

**Use.** A seasonal chapter as a way to introduce crops, and an honest closed state. Editorial type next to a lot of air.

**Leave.** The script logo, the membership ladder, the event commerce, and any layout that dies without photography. The seasonal row is adapted as a single real crop, not three invented ones.

## Limits of this pass

Two Brothers: no full product-detail page, and no separate phone viewport. The hamburger was already the desktop navigation. Hexhamshire: the box product page was not given a full visual pass. Winterspring: article pages were titled on the homepage and not opened. Garden Sweet: no separate phone viewport. `gardensweetfarm.com` returned HTTP 500. The working site is `gardensweet.com`.

## What Ram Ganga Agro takes

A short nav. A type-led first screen. One calm action pair, neither of which is Shop. A chaptered farm story that admits missing facts. One crop, with a status instead of a price. A journal that can be empty. A follow form that says what the note contains. A future harvest page in the model, not on the nav. Photography as a slot, the way Hexhamshire’s grey box already is.
