# 07 — Our Farm

Three child pages plus a parent. All of them are paper, prose width 680px for the essay, with the page title at the 1120px container. Breadcrumb under the header. No photography until the farm provides it. Do not insert the panicle on every page. The parent may use it once, small, beside the title. The children are type.

Shared chrome: header, breadcrumb, footer. One `h1`. A text link back to the parent at the end of each child.

## `/our-farm`

**Visitor.** Someone who clicked Explore our farm, wanting to know if this is a real place.

**Order.** Title. Three short chapters, stacked, each with an `h2` and at most 80 words. A link on each to the child page.

1. Our story — DRAFT: The people and the reason for the farm will be told here by the farm. Until then, this page will not guess.
2. The land — DRAFT: The fields have a place. The site will name it when the farm does.
3. Practices — DRAFT: How the crop is grown will be described in the farm’s words, and only then.

**Desktop.** Title left, the three chapters in one column, not three cards. 32px between chapters. A hairline between them.

**Mobile.** The same stack. Title uses the page-title clamp.

**Empty.** This page is the empty state for the biography. It should look deliberate. It should not say “lorem” or “coming soon” three times in badges.

**Farm must supply.** Who farms, since when, and a sentence they are willing to sign. Optional: a portrait with consent.

## `/our-farm/story`

**h1:** Our story.

If no biography has been supplied, the body is one paragraph: The story of Ram Ganga Agro will be published in the farm’s own words. Then a link to What we grow, which is known.

If a biography is supplied, set it as paragraphs, then an optional 4/5 image with a caption and alt written by the farm. No pull-quote unless the farm wrote it.

Do not add a timeline with invented years.

## `/our-farm/the-land`

**h1:** The land.

Body, until a location is supplied: The land will be described when the farm is ready to say where it is.

When supplied, the page may include the place name they approve, the character of the soil or water only if they wrote it, and one photograph. No embedded map that drops a pin they have not approved. No acreage unless they provide a figure they will stand behind.

## `/our-farm/practices`

**h1:** Practices.

Body, until methods are supplied: Farming practices will be described here only when the farm has confirmed them.

When supplied, one `h2` per practice they name, each with a short paragraph. No icons of leaves. No comparison with “chemical farming” unless they wrote that sentence. No certification logo.

## Accessibility and links

Breadcrumb: Our Farm, or Our Farm / The land. The current crumb is not a link. Child pages are in the footer only as part of Our Farm. They do not each become a nav item.
