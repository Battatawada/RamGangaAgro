# 31 — Farm content approval

One list of what the public site says, and what it is still waiting to say. Replace a row when the farm decides. Do not fill a gap with a likely sentence.

Status values:

- **Approved** — may be read as the farm’s statement.
- **Draft** — on the site, from the page specifications, waiting for the farm.
- **Placeholder** — an honest gap. It is not a fact.
- **Not yet available** — omitted, or the slot is null. Do not publish a guess.

The four crop sentences below are the safe public set from the project brief of 13 November 2026. The spelling of Raktshali is still worth a deliberate confirmation if the farm spells it differently. Everything else on the farm pages is draft or a placeholder.

## Photographs still to make

None of these exist in the repository. Each file needs alt text from someone who was there. A person who is recognisable needs a recorded yes. The site does not assume it.

- A wide view of the land, with sky and field, in morning or late light.
- The same view closer, with plants in the foreground.
- The crop in the field. Do not grade an unconfirmed colour into the picture.
- Dried grain on a surface the farm actually uses, when there is grain to show.
- Soil, water, or the edge of a field.
- Hands and tools at work, where a person is comfortable being shown.
- Seasonal changes of the same view, when the seasons have been recorded.
- Harvest: cutting, gathering, or drying, only if and when that work happens on the farm.
- Natural details: a stem, water, soil. Not a styled dish, and not a mock pack.

## Film, later

No video is published, and none should be implied. When the farm wants YouTube or a social clip, useful shapes are:

- A field walk.
- A short crop update.
- A seasonal observation.
- A harvest journey, once there is a harvest.
- A farm-to-table story, once the farm wants to tell one.

A hero film on the site needs a poster image, a pause control, no autoplay when reduced motion is requested, and captions if anyone speaks.

## A practical way to collect the next update

Ask for one thing at a time, in this order:

1. Confirm the spelling of Raktshali, or correct it.
2. Say whether any of the draft home and farm sentences should be replaced, or may stay until a real paragraph exists.
3. Give a public email if one should be printed. Leave it off otherwise.
4. Name a place only if it may be published.
5. Send one wide photograph and one crop photograph, with alt text.
6. Have the reviewer read the privacy draft and the terms draft before the domain serves them.

Do not send a questionnaire that asks for acreage, yield, certifications, and a founding story in the same pass.
