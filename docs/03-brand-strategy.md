# 03 — Brand strategy

## Position

Ram Ganga Agro is the digital home of a working farm. It is not an organic supermarket, and it is not a campaign. The first visit should answer who is farming, what is growing, and why a person might one day want that harvest. It should not answer with a discount.

The line, pending approval:

> Good food begins long before the table.

The name is set in small tracked capitals. The sentence does the work.

## Feeling

Calm, warm, specific, modern, and editorial. Premium in the sense of care and space, not in the sense of a luxury goods site. Rooted, without kraft paper, leaf icons, or a green wash.

## Voice

Short sentences. Concrete when the fact is the farm’s. Silent when it is not. No exclamation marks. No “100% organic.” No “shop now.” No testimonials until a real person has agreed to be quoted, and even then not on the homepage in Phase 1.

Draft copy in the page specs is marked DRAFT. It is a direction for tone. The farm replaces any sentence that overclaims.

## Name and mark

Wordmark: RAM GANGA AGRO, Fraunces, tracked. The mark is the panicle in `design/illustrations/mark.svg`. It is a drawing of grain, used small. It does not replace the wordmark in the header.

## Colour story

Paper, like the surface food is served on. Ink, like print. Green, like a crop seen from a distance, used for structure. Burgundy, like the rice, used once per view. Grain gold is a metal in the drawing, not a text colour. The measured ratios are in `design/colors.json`. Grain on paper is 2.04:1 and must not be text.

## Why this type

Fraunces is a soft optical serif. It can feel agricultural without becoming a script or a western display face. Outfit is plain and slightly geometric, readable at small sizes, and not Inter, which Garden Sweet already uses for UI. Playfair and Lora were set aside because the references already lean on them and they read as a template.

## What the brand refuses

A leaf logo. A badge row. A cartoon farmer. A ticker. A grid of products the farm does not grow. Health claims about the rice. A certification mark of any kind until the farm holds that certificate and asks for it to be shown.
